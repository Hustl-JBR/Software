# Carrier sourcing

Carrier options are businesses, not drivers. Each option records source/call evidence, offered rate in dollars, authority and insurance assertions, cargo coverage, blockers, and status. Selection requires authorized human action, sufficient recorded evidence, idempotency, and audit.

Only after selecting a carrier does Atlas record dispatcher, driver, tractor, trailer, and tracking contacts. Owner-operators still pass through the carrier-business selection boundary. Future load-board/compliance providers augment evidence; they do not bypass the decision command.
