# Freight technology map

Atlas owns organization membership, intake revisions, approvals, loads/stops, ownership, quote decisions, carrier-selection evidence, task/communication history, and audit. External systems may later supply load-board capacity/rates, carrier authority/compliance, GPS/ELD position, documents, messaging, accounting, and payments.

Each external boundary needs a provider-neutral adapter, evidence timestamp/source, failure and stale-data states, manual fallback, scoped credentials, audit, and contract tests. A provider's marketing category must never be confused with verified behavior.
