# DAT integration plan

DAT is planning-only. Potential future use cases are lane-market context, load posting/search, carrier responses, and recorded negotiation evidence. Before implementation Atlas needs official documentation, commercial access, credential/security terms, rate limits, data retention rights, sandbox behavior, and precise permitted actions.

The adapter should expose provider-neutral search/post/update/close operations and preserve raw evidence metadata without making DAT authoritative for Atlas load, quote, or carrier-selection state. Human review remains required. No DAT credentials, calls, or simulated connection are present.
